% ---------- Current & Ongoing Research Projects ----------
\cvsection{Current \& Ongoing Research Projects}

\researchproject
  {TAPS / Belonging-Centered Instruction (BCI)}
  {Research focus within the TAPS project}
  {Develop computational methods to identify and model instructional practices that support students' sense of belonging and safety in classroom and tutoring interactions. This work focuses on analyzing teacher--student discourse at both the utterance and session level and generating scalable feedback to help educators understand and improve their instructional practices.}

\researchproject
  {Connection Finder}
  {Research focus within the NSF Institute for Student-AI Teaming (iSAT)}
  {Develop computational methods for analyzing collaborative student dialogue and linking student contributions to instructional learning goals. This project combines dependency dialogue analysis, curriculum and lesson material informed contextual paraphrasing, and learning goal classification to help educators understand how students make progress toward learning goals and facilitate more productive classroom discussions.}

\researchproject
  {Multimodal Analysis of Classroom Instruction}
  {Area exam research direction}
  {Investigate how multimodal classroom signals, including spoken discourse, whiteboard content, written annotations, gestures, and other instructional artifacts can be jointly modeled to better understand instructional quality and student engagement. A longer-term goal of this work is to develop annotation schemas and taxonomies that capture how verbal, visual, and interactional signals relate to one another, along with computational models that can learn these cross-modal relationships. These representations and models could support downstream tasks such as identifying pedagogical moves by teachers and students, tracing how instructional actions unfold across modalities, and developing richer, context-aware analyses of teaching and learning.}
